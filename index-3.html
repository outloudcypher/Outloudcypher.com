Who We Are

Outloud Cypher is a hip-hop community where rappers, lyricists, beatboxers and poets step into one circle and let their voices out without fear. Small stage or big, the energy is always huge.

Mission

Platform for Talent

A place for underground artists to be heard.

Community

Learn from each other, collaborate, grow together.

Culture

The real roots of hip-hop: cypher, freestyle, storytelling.

Cypher Rules

01

Respect the Circle

When someone is speaking, give them your full attention.
02

Be Original

Your story, your bars, your style.
03

Good Vibes Only

Cypher or battle, it is about love, not hate.

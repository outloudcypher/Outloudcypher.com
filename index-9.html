Who We Are

Outloud Cypher is a hip-hop community where rappers, lyricists, beatboxers and poets step into one circle and let their voices out without fear. Small stage or big, the energy is always huge.

Mission

Platform for Talent

A place for underground artists to be heard.

Community

Learn from each other, collaborate, grow together.

Culture

The real roots of hip-hop: cypher, freestyle, storytelling.

Cypher Rules

FAQ

Is it free to join?

Register on the website and you will get your pass number instantly. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

Do I have to perform?

No. Come to rap, spit poetry, beatbox, or just feel the energy as part of the crowd.

Who can join?

Anyone with love for hip-hop. Beginner or pro, the circle is open.
